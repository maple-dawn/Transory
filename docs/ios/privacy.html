Transory for iPhone Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Transory is a translation app that uses AI services you configure yourself (for example DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, or a local model running on your own network). Transory has no servers of its own, and the developer does not collect, store, or view any of your content.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Content: the text you type or paste for translation, and text recognized on your device from photos you take. Photos themselves are never uploaded.
  • Recipients: only the AI providers you add, configure with your own API key, and explicitly authorize on the data-sharing consent screen. Providers you have not authorized receive nothing.
  • How: content is sent directly from your device to the provider over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), never through any server operated by the developer. When you use a local model on your network, content stays on your local network.
  • Provider handling: each provider processes content under its own privacy policy, linked from the consent screen and from Settings › Data & Privacy.

Data stored on your device

  • API keys are stored in the device Keychain and used only to call the matching provider.
  • Translation history and favorites stay on your device and can be deleted at any time.
  • Settings (services, scenes, consent status) are stored locally.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or device identifiers.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Camera and local network

The camera is used only when you choose photo translation; text recognition runs on device. Local network access is used only to reach Ollama or LM Studio running on your own computer.

Your choices

You can revoke any provider's authorization in Settings › Data & Privacy › AI Service Data Authorization, or turn on "Confirm before each send" to review the text and recipients before every translation.

Children

Transory is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if this policy changes. For questions, contact us via GitHub Issues.

Transory for iPhone 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

Transory 是一款翻译 App，使用你自己配置的 AI 服务（例如 DeepSeek、Claude、Gemini、OpenAI，或你在局域网中运行的本地模型）进行翻译。Transory 没有自己的服务器，开发者不会收集、存储或查看你的任何内容。

我们发送什么、发给谁

  • 发送的内容：你输入或粘贴的要翻译的文字，以及拍照后在设备上识别出的文字。照片本身不会上传。
  • 接收方：只有你在 App 中添加、填写了 API Key、并在「数据发送同意」页面勾选授权的 AI 服务商。没有授权的服务不会收到任何内容。
  • 发送方式：内容通过加密连接（HTTPS）从你的设备直接发送给服务商，不经过开发者的任何服务器。连接局域网中的本地模型时，内容只在你的本地网络中传输。
  • 服务商如何处理：由各服务商按照其自己的隐私政策处理。你可以在 App 的同意页面或「设置 › 数据与隐私」中查看各服务商的隐私政策链接。

保存在你设备上的数据

  • API Key：保存在设备的钥匙串（Keychain）中，只用于向对应服务商发起请求。
  • 翻译历史与收藏：只保存在你的设备上，你可以随时在「历史」中删除。
  • 设置：服务列表、使用场景和授权状态保存在设备本地。

删除 App 会同时删除以上本地数据（钥匙串中的 API Key 可能由系统保留到下次重装，你可以在删除前于设置中清除）。

我们不做的事

  • 不收集分析数据、崩溃日志或设备标识符。
  • 不使用广告，不跨 App 或网站追踪你。
  • 不出售或共享你的数据。

相机与本地网络

相机只在你使用「拍照翻译」时启用，文字识别在设备上完成。本地网络权限只用于连接你自己在同一网络中运行的 Ollama 或 LM Studio。

你的选择

你可以随时在「设置 › 数据与隐私 › AI 服务数据授权」中撤回对任一服务的授权，或打开「每次发送前确认」，在每次翻译前确认要发送的内容和接收方。

儿童

Transory 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更与联系方式

本政策如有变更，会更新此页面并修改生效日期。如有问题，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们。
